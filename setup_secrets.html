Copy into Streamlit

  1. Open your app's Advanced settings or Settings, then find Secrets.
  2. Copy the entire block below and paste it into Secrets.
  3. Replace any GitHub placeholders that remain, then save the settings.
  4. Keep a secure copy of the encryption key so you can reopen your saved data later.
